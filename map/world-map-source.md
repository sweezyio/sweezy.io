# World map attribution

`world.svg` is adapted from [BlankMap-World-Sovereign Nations](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BlankMap-World-Sovereign_Nations.svg), by RedGolpe, with updates by WikiPate.

License: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The modified SVG remains under this license.

Changes: light-gray land, dark borders, transparent water and background, gray United States, and a descriptive title. Original country geometry and small-country markers are preserved.

To mark another country visited, change the fill on its country group and descendant paths to `#808080` (some paths have explicit fills).
